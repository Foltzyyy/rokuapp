# Privacy Policy — The Chapel Littlestown Roku app

*Draft for the church website. Roku requires a privacy policy link for every app in its store. Have someone at the church review it before posting.*

---

**The Chapel Littlestown app for Roku**

Last updated: [date]

The Chapel Littlestown app lets you watch our live services and past services on your Roku device. We want you to know exactly what it does with information.

**What we collect**
Nothing. The app has no accounts, asks for no personal information, and doesn't use advertising or analytics.

**What stays on your Roku**
So you can pick up where you left off, the app remembers which past services you've started and how far you got. This is stored only on your Roku device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

**Video from Vimeo**
Our services are hosted on Vimeo. When you watch, your Roku connects to Vimeo to play the video, the same as watching on our website. Vimeo's privacy policy (vimeo.com/privacy) covers that connection.

**Roku**
Roku may collect information about how its devices and apps are used, under Roku's own privacy policy (roku.com/legal).

**Questions**
Contact us through thechapel.info.
